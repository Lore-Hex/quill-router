Get listed: two headers, no application.

Send your requests through a Confidential route and name your app. HTTP-Referer is your app's unique id (its URL); X-NYTE-Title is the name we show. X-OpenRouter-Title and X-Title work too. Your app appears in the verified ranking within 15 minutes of its first encrypted request.

Already listed on the watchlist or as self-attested? Route through the exchange, then claim your listing and we will move it up to Verified encrypted. To hide your app from the ranking, or to claim and correct a listing, email help@trustedrouter.com.

How a listing is verified

Attested gateway

The request terminates in an enclave whose code measurement is published and checked by anyone at /trust.

Attested provider

Confidential routes run on provider hardware with its own attestation, never on a host that can read the prompt.

Signed receipts

Any response can carry a receipt binding the request to both attestations. Verify one yourself.
