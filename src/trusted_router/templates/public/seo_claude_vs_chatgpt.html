Which draft would you send?

Claude vs ChatGPT for writing

Use a real brief: rewrite a product announcement for customers, shorten an internal memo and summarize a supplied document. Require both candidates to preserve names, dates and numbers, and mark missing facts instead of inventing them. Hide the model names before reviewing the drafts. Score factual fidelity, tone, structure and minutes of human editing.

The best AI model for writing is the one that meets your editorial standard consistently. A persuasive first paragraph is not enough if the rest introduces unsupported claims. Keep a fast, inexpensive candidate in the comparison for routine rewrites and reserve a more costly candidate for difficult synthesis when your results justify it.

Claude vs GPT: which is better for coding?

Use a small bug fix, a cross-file change and a test-writing task. Compare the complete agent loop, including tool calls and failed attempts. A model that explains code well may still make a poor autonomous editor. Our coding-model guide gives a repeatable scorecard and includes DeepSeek and Kimi as additional candidates.
