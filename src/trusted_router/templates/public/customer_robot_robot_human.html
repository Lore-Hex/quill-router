The customer

Legal reasoning over documents that cannot be treated casually.

Robot, Robot & Human is a legal AI platform. It ingests litigation productions, performs OCR and text extraction, embeds the resulting material, and runs multi-stage reasoning to produce searchable evidence, timelines, contradiction analysis, and attorney work product.

The inputs include opposing-party productions, privileged communications, and matters under active litigation. The team needed production-scale inference without building its own GPU fleet or maintaining a separate integration for every model provider.

The implementation

One API, explicit route policy, and an attested gateway.

RRH kept its OpenAI-shaped application calls and moved the base URL to TrustedRouter. It could use lower-cost models for extraction and stronger models for synthesis and verification while keeping routing, provider fallback, billing, and metadata accounting behind one interface.

Every request entered through TrustedRouter's attested confidential-compute gateway. TrustedRouter does not log or store prompt or output content. RRH can verify the running gateway image against the published source and choose ZDR or independently verified end-to-end confidential provider routes where the workload requires them.

Trust boundary
Gateway attestation and provider guarantees answer different questions.

The live attestation proves which TrustedRouter gateway workload is receiving the request and that it matches the published image. Downstream model handling remains route-specific and is published on each model and provider page. Use ZDR routes for contractual zero retention and independently verified E2E routes when the provider's model-serving boundary must also be confidential.
