Continuously sampled from TrustedRouter's monitor regions using a {{ snapshot.window_label or "recent live sample" }}. Time-to-first-token (TTFT), time-to-first-byte (TTFB), effective visible throughput, and pinned route success come from real streaming requests, not vendor claims. Pinned route success measures the exact provider and model requested, without fallback. Effective throughput uses provider-reported visible completion tokens divided by complete request time. Reported reasoning tokens are separated, and samples that exceed the requested completion-token cap are rejected. Unsupported routes and probe-configuration rows are reported separately. No prompt or output content is ever stored.
