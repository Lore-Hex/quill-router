Minimum charges and per-request rounding

Eligible standard routes use the prices in effect when the request was authorized, with the rounding and minimum below. Streamed usage for these routes includes cost_microdollars.

Standard prepaid text and embedding usage is billed in whole microdollars: 1 microdollar = $0.000001. Here is how the charge is calculated:

  1. Start with the selected route's retail token rates and applicable context-length tier. Standard retail rates are provider cost plus 5.5%, rounded up to a whole microdollar per million tokens, with a $0.01 per million token price floor. The published retail rates already include this markup and price floor; do not add 5.5% again.
  2. Multiply each component's token count by its rate per million tokens, then divide by 1,000,000. Round input, output, cache read, and cache write separately to whole microdollars, half up (0.5 rounds to 1; 1.5 rounds to 2). Cached tokens use their applicable cache rates, without counting them again as ordinary input.
  3. Add the rounded components and any fixed per-request fee. Apply a 1 microdollar minimum per billed request whenever there is positive usage at a positive rate or a positive request fee, even if all token components round to zero. With no chargeable usage and no fee, the charge is zero.

Route-specific fees and minimums can increase the total: Perplexity routes include a per-request fee, and Parasail Liberty 2.0 has a $0.001 minimum per top-level request. Check the selected route's pricing on the models page. Video jobs use their separate quoted billing described in the video guide.
