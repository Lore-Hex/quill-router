    How this estimate works

    Eligible spend = monthly spend × eligible share. Exchange provider cost = eligible spend × exchange price percentage. Add 5.5% to that provider cost, then add the spend that stays with your current provider. Annual savings are monthly savings × 12.

    This is an illustration, not a quote or a measured customer result. The example assumes 40% of spend can move to providers priced at 10% of your current price at the same token volume. Lower prices do not imply equal model quality. Evaluate your own workload first.

    USD estimates exclude tax, payment fees, the per token price floor, commitments, migration costs and changes in input, output or cache mix. No inference calls are made.
