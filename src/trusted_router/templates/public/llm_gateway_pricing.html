For a known single-endpoint charge from {{ api_base_url }}, the documented TrustedRouter usage fields have this shape (illustrative values):

{"usage":{"prompt_tokens":100,"completion_tokens":20,"total_tokens":120,"cost_microdollars":1000,"total_cost_microdollars":1000}}

cost_microdollars reports the known request charge in integer microdollars; total_cost_microdollars is the same charge for this single-endpoint response, not an additional fee. One million microdollars equals $1.00; unknown cost is omitted.

  1. Response usage: streaming chat includes cost with stream_options.include_usage: true.
  2. Activity: GET /v1/activity?group_by=none lists generations; tags allocate spend.
  3. Durable billing: GET /v1/requests/{gateway_request_id}/billing returns the settlement status.
  4. Signed receipts: verify hashes and route integrity offline.

Billing details

Standard billing rounds each input, output, cache-read and cache-write component to whole microdollars, half up, with a one-microdollar ($0.000001) minimum for chargeable usage.

The token price floor is $0.01 per million tokens; route-specific minimums and request fees may add more. Signed receipts are an optional service with a 12% total fee for managed prepaid inference, replacing the standard {{ cost_guide.fee_percent }}%. See billing rules. Video has separate quoted pricing.

Buyer checklist

Collect five numbers before choosing: monthly input/output millions, cache-hit share, premium-retry share, the fee percentage and its basis, and monthly infrastructure or subscription cost.
