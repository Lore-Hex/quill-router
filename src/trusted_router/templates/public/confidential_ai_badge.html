Privacy with proof

A trust seal backed by a running system.

The badge says that the AI feature displaying it sends requests through TrustedRouter's hardware-attested gateway and enforces the confidential provider route. TrustedRouter never logs or stores prompt or output content.

Use model="trustedrouter/confidential", model="trustedrouter/e2e", or provider.min_privacy="confidential". These routes fail closed when no qualifying confidential endpoint is available.

The badge is evidence of the AI data path. It is not a SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, or product-wide certification.
