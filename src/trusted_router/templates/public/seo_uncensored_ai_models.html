What does uncensored mean?

It is a broad label for models marketed with reduced content restrictions or refusal behavior. It is not a standardized capability or quality score. A model can still refuse a request, make mistakes or have hosting restrictions.

Abliterated describes a modification intended to change refusal behavior. Open weights describes access to model parameters. Neither term establishes zero retention, encryption or a particular license.
