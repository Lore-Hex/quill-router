Group purchasing

Small buyers become one big buyer.

Cloud discounts favor scale. Most startups and independent teams cannot negotiate like a company spending a million dollars every month. TrustedRouter collects the commitments, signs the large Bedrock agreement, and passes the group pricing through to participants. The first target is 10% savings at $1 million per month. More committed volume can support a deeper negotiated discount.

  1. 01
    Pledge privately

    Tell TrustedRouter what your team can commit for 12 months. Nothing identifying is published.

  2. 02
    Reach $1M per month

    The public counter combines the founding commitments and new online pledges. It can grow beyond the goal.

  3. 03
    Review final terms

    TrustedRouter negotiates the agreement. Participants receive final terms before signing or spending.

  4. 04
    Keep 10%

    Once the group agreement closes, eligible Bedrock usage receives the group savings through TrustedRouter.
