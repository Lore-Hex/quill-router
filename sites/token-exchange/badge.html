What the badge means

Membership is the mark.
Evidence is the standard.

The NYTE badge is for active providers and enterprise members whose participation has been confirmed by the exchange. Downloading a badge does not establish membership. If you are applying or evaluating the exchange, wait for confirmation before displaying it.

Membership does not mean every model or request uses confidential computing, zero retention, or a particular processing location. This static badge is not a live membership check, an independent security audit, or a product-wide certification.

For confidential AI claims, use the TrustedRouter Confidential AI badge only on eligible routes. Review the trust center and technical evidence for the actual data path.

Wear it well.

Keep it linked. Link to this page so customers can understand the mark. Do not turn it into a blanket “AI-secure” certification.

Keep it legible. Use the supplied colors and proportions, at least 240 pixels wide, with clear space around it. Do not crop or alter the words.

Keep it accurate. Use it only while your membership is active and only for your confirmed role. Remove it if participation ends.
