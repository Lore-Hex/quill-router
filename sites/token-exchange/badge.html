What the badge tells your customers

An exchange buyer.
A visible commitment.

The NYTE badge is exclusively for active enterprise buyers whose participation has been confirmed by the exchange, to display to their customers. It is not a supplier or provider badge. Downloading a badge does not establish membership. If you are applying or evaluating the exchange, wait for confirmation before displaying it.

Membership does not mean every model or request uses confidential computing, zero retention, or a particular processing location. This static badge is not a live membership check, an independent security audit, or a product-wide certification.

For confidential AI claims, use the TrustedRouter Confidential AI badge only on eligible routes. Review the trust center and technical evidence for the actual data path.

Wear it well.

Keep it linked. Link to this page so your customers can understand the mark. Do not turn it into a blanket “AI-secure” certification.

Keep it legible. Use the supplied colors and proportions, at least 240 pixels wide, with clear space around it. Do not crop or alter the words.

Keep it accurate. Use it only while your membership is active as a confirmed enterprise buyer. Remove it if participation ends.
