What the lock means

The NYTE standard.
Confidential, by requirement.

NYTE requires every AI route to meet its confidential routing standard. This seal is exclusively for enterprise buyers to display to their customers on NYTE-powered AI features. It is not a supplier badge, and downloading the artwork does not verify your product.

The required data path uses TrustedRouter’s hardware-attested gateway and enforces model="trustedrouter/confidential", model="trustedrouter/e2e", or provider.min_privacy="confidential". The same route must have verified confidential compute, provider-side E2EE, and explicit zero data retention; requests must fail closed rather than fall back to an ineligible endpoint.

The encryption claim is route-specific: client TLS terminates inside the attested gateway, and provider-side end-to-end encryption is a separate verified property. The seal does not claim that the gateway cannot process plaintext, or that unrelated features on your website are end-to-end encrypted. Customer-side logging, processing location, and other systems require their own review.

This static image is not a live verification result, an independent security audit, or a product-wide certification. Review the confidential AI routing standard and the route-specific evidence; verify the live gateway to inspect the privacy claim.

Wear it well.

Keep it linked. Link to this page so customers can inspect the privacy claim and evidence. Place it beside the eligible AI feature, or clearly identify that feature in your trust center.

Keep it legible. Use the supplied colors and proportions, at least 240 pixels wide, with clear space around it. Do not crop or alter the words.

Keep it accurate. Display it only while the feature enforces every route requirement. Remove it if that changes. Do not present it as whole-company or whole-product certification.
