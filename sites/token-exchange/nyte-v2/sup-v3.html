Common questions

What do we need to start?

A working model endpoint. Share your infrastructure and available capacity, and we’ll plan the integration with you.

Do we keep our brand?

Yes. You sell inference under your own provider name, and buyers see it on every route you serve.

How do we qualify for confidential routes?

Supported hardware, a verified serving runtime, independent attestation and a zero-retention policy. The route goes live once all four are verified.

How do listing and payouts work?

Listing and payout terms are agreed during onboarding.
