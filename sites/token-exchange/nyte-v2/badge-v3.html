What the badge means

The NYTE standard.
Confidential, by requirement.

Who may show it

NYTE requires every AI route to meet its confidential routing standard. This badge is for enterprise buyers to display to their customers on NYTE-powered AI features. A supplier may display it only where NYTE publishes an evidence page for that supplier’s verified route, as it does for Maple, and only linked to that page. Downloading the artwork does not verify your product.

What the route must enforce

The required data path uses TrustedRouter’s hardware-attested gateway and enforces one of:

  • model="trustedrouter/confidential"
  • model="trustedrouter/e2e"
  • provider.min_privacy="confidential"

The same route must have verified confidential compute, provider-side E2EE, and explicit zero data retention; requests must fail closed rather than fall back to an ineligible endpoint.

What the encryption claim covers

The encryption claim is route-specific: client TLS terminates inside the attested gateway, and provider-side end-to-end encryption is a separate verified property. The badge does not claim that the gateway cannot process plaintext, or that unrelated features on your website are end-to-end encrypted. Customer-side logging, processing location, and other systems require their own review.

What the badge is not

This static image is not a live verification result, an independent security audit, or a product-wide certification. Review the confidential AI routing standard and the route-specific evidence; verify the live gateway to inspect the privacy claim.

Using the badge

Wear it well.

Keep it linked.

Link to this page so customers can inspect the privacy claim and evidence. Place it beside the eligible AI feature, or clearly identify that feature in your trust center.

Keep it legible.

Use the supplied files and proportions, at least 64 pixels tall, with clear space around it. Do not crop, recolour or alter the words.

Keep it accurate.

Display it only while the feature enforces every route requirement. Remove it if that changes. Do not present it as whole-company or whole-product certification.
