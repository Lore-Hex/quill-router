Common questions

What happens when no route qualifies?

The request is refused with an error. It is never sent to a provider that misses your requirements.

Does New York mean our data stays in New York?

The name is the market. Where requests run is set by your route rules. Restrict to US-hosted providers in one setting.

Do we need a new account?

No. Your TrustedRouter account, balance and key limits carry over. Set approved routes and spending limits before anything reaches production.

Which models run on confidential routes?

The models above, with more added as providers qualify. The live catalog shows every route and its protections.

How do we run a pilot?

Bring one representative workload. Over 30 days we compare quality, cost and privacy on your own task set, then set your approved routes and spending limits for production.

Is this a securities or crypto exchange?

No. NYTE sells AI inference, billed by the token.
