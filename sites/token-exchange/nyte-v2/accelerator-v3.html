We bring the software and the market.

NYTE supplies Neocloud-in-a-box, the Apache-2.0 open-source neocloud and inference stack. The software is free to use and pre-alpha. Run it yourself, or discuss having it remotely managed by NYTE. Remote management is an offer to discuss, not a live product.

NYTE connects you with demand from buyers on the exchange, with metering and billing through TrustedRouter. Hardware, deployment responsibilities and operating terms are scoped together. Confidential serving requires independent hardware and software qualification, not merely installing the software.
