Reserved tokens terms

This card purchase is a reservation for tokens delivered in 2027. It is a prepaid inference service, not a security or investment.

What you buy

One lot is 1 billion tokens of one NYTE Index grade: AAA, A, B or C. All four grades can be reserved by card. Specific models, throughput and planned facilities are discussed by call and are not sold by card.

Price and quantity

The per-lot price is the selected grade’s dated NYTE Index price less the early-reservation discount shown at checkout. It is rounded to whole US cents, with half a cent rounded up. The total is the per-lot price multiplied by the number of lots. Your price is locked at purchase.

You may buy at least one lot and up to the card checkout maximum shown with your quote. Larger orders are discussed by call.

Delivery and expiry

Delivery is through a TrustedRouter account via the NYTE API. Tokens are usable January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2027. Unused tokens expire December 31, 2027.

Refunds

You may request a full refund any time before January 1, 2027. To request it, contact enterprise@trustedrouter.com with your Stripe payment reference.
