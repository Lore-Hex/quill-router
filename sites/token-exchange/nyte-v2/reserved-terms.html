Reserved tokens terms

This card purchase is a reservation for tokens delivered in 2027. It is a prepaid inference service, not a security or investment.

What you buy

One lot is 1 billion tokens of one NYTE Index grade: AAA, A, B or C. All four grades can be reserved by card. Specific models, throughput and planned facilities are discussed by call and are not sold by card.

Price and quantity

The per-lot price is the selected grade’s dated NYTE Index price less the 10% early-reservation discount shown at checkout. It is rounded to whole US cents, with half a cent rounded up. The total is the per-lot price multiplied by the number of lots. Your price is locked at purchase.

You may buy at least one lot and up to the card checkout maximum shown with your quote. Larger orders are discussed by call.

Delivery and expiry

Delivery is through a TrustedRouter account via the NYTE API. Tokens are usable January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2027. Unused tokens expire December 31, 2027.

Refunds

You may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, the only way to exit your reservation is through NYTE’s resale program. To request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, contact enterprise@trustedrouter.com with your position ID or Stripe payment reference.

Transfers and resale

Reservations are transferable only through NYTE’s resale program. The holder lists lots, and NYTE resells them to new buyers ahead of new inventory. On each sale, the holder receives the resale price minus a 5% resale fee, refunded to the original card and capped at what the holder paid for those lots.

Until self-service resale launches, resale requests are handled by email to enterprise@trustedrouter.com, the same address used for refund requests. Include your position ID or Stripe payment reference and the lots you want to list.

Your position

Every completed purchase is a separate position with a stable ID. NYTE records that ID with the Stripe purchase reference so operations can identify your reservation. Keep your Stripe receipt for refund or resale requests.
