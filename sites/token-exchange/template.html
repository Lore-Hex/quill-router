Before you begin

Good questions.

What exactly am I buying?

Access to model inference through TrustedRouter. AI tokens measure model inputs and outputs. This is an inference service, not a securities or cryptocurrency exchange.

Does this market guarantee local processing?

The regional name identifies the audience for this page. Processing location, residency and provider eligibility depend on the route and agreed terms. Confirm these requirements with us before sending sensitive data.

Can we start with one workload?

Yes. Bring a representative, non-sensitive task set and expected volume. We can compare suitable models and providers, then define quality, cost and privacy requirements for a pilot.

I run inference. Can I sell capacity here?

We onboard inference providers with working model endpoints. Share your infrastructure and available capacity so we can discuss integration. Listing and purchase volumes are subject to review and demand.
