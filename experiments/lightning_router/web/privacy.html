Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

Scope

Lore Hex Corp operates LightningRouter. This page describes the Lightning funding website; the TrustedRouter Privacy Policy also applies to the underlying API. You do not need to provide an email, username or password to fund a key. This is not a promise of anonymity.

Keys and payment records

Your browser keeps checkout state and your key in this tab's session storage so you can resume a payment. The funding service receives your key to authenticate balance reads and bind payments. It stores a protected key fingerprint and temporarily encrypts a new pending key so a settled payment can be credited even if the browser disconnects. That pending ciphertext is removed after account binding; canceled, unpaid checkouts are pruned on a bounded schedule.

We keep the invoice identifier, payment hash, BTC amount, exchange rate, USD credit amount, settlement status and accounting linkage needed to credit payments once, reconcile failures, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Payment networks and your wallet provider have their own records and policies. Funding records may link activity to an existing account when you supply its key.

Inference and usage

Inference goes to TrustedRouter's attested API, separately from this funding website. Ordinary synchronous and streaming prompts and outputs are not retained as TrustedRouter logs. Billing and operational metadata, including model, provider, token counts, cost and request identifiers, are retained as described in the API privacy policy. Optional Batch and content-export features have their own disclosed handling rules.

The selected model provider processes your request under its own terms. Private routing does not mean every provider offers zero retention or attested model execution. Review provider policies and verify gateway attestation.

Website security and third parties

This funding site uses essential session storage and does not load third-party advertising scripts. Infrastructure and edge security services can process connection metadata such as IP addresses and request paths. We use Coinbase's public exchange-rate feed for conversion quotes without sending it your API key. Our infrastructure providers process information needed to host, secure and operate the service; see the subprocessor list.

Retention, rights and contact

We retain billing and security records as needed for accounting, fraud prevention, disputes, legal obligations and service operation. We do not promise that deleting a browser session erases those records. Processing may occur in the United States and other countries. For applicable access, correction, deletion or privacy requests, contact security@trustedrouter.com. We may need to verify control of the relevant account without asking you to email a raw API key.
