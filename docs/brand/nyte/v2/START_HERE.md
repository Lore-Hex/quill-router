## Start designing with Claude (5 minutes)

1. **Add the skill.** In Claude, go to *Customize → Skills*, click **+**, choose *Create skill → Upload a skill*, and upload `02_Claude_Kit/nyte-brand-skill.zip`. (Skills need *Create and edit files* / code execution turned on in *Settings → Capabilities*.) Claude will then load the NYTE rules, tokens, assets and fonts whenever a request mentions NYTE.
2. **Create a Project** called "NYTE". Paste `02_Claude_Kit/Claude_Project_Instructions.md` into the Project instructions, and upload everything in `02_Claude_Kit/Project_Knowledge/` as Project knowledge.
3. **Ask for work** inside that Project. `02_Claude_Kit/Starter_Prompts.md` has ready-made starting points for decks, web sections, ads, one-pagers and documents.

Either step 1 or step 2 works on its own; together they give the best results. Full details are in `02_Claude_Kit/README.md`.

## Before anything ships

- **Fonts:** the Pelikan files here are Dinamo **trial** files, for design and approval only. License ABC Pelikan, Pelikan Condensed and Pelikan Mono from Dinamo (abcdinamo.com) before any public use.
- **Badge naming:** the system uses "Verified Secure"; the current media kit says "Privacy Secured seal". Confirm one name.
- **Mission line:** "Make inference a market buyers can see into, before the first request." is new copy for approval.
- **Photography:** the photos in the guidelines and reference work are placeholders from the photo library. Clear rights and consent before publishing.
- **Placeholders:** anything in [brackets] is unverified and must be confirmed or removed.
