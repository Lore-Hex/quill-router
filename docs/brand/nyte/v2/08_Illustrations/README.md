# Illustrations

Ten 480 × 336 illustrations on a 24 px grid. A circle is a place, a line is a flow (parallel lines slanted like the mark's speed lines), and NYTE is always the square at the center. 6 px lines with round caps; no Band or stepped stripes. Each sits on its own ground (Sky, Cream, Purple, Yellow, Ink, Lime) with Ink and at most two brand colors, and carries an `aria-label`. Use one per idea, at full width of a column or slide; never crop, recolor or combine them. New illustrations follow the rules in the brand book, section 8.
