# NYTE · AI brief

Paste this into any AI tool before asking it to write or design for NY Token Exchange. It is the short form of the NYTE Brand Guidelines V2 (Nov 2026). Use it together with `nyte.tokens.json` or `nyte-tokens.css` and the asset files. If a request conflicts with a constraint below, follow the constraint and say so.

## Context

- NY Token Exchange is **the marketplace for buyers and sellers of inference**: buyers compare models, operators, regions, data handling and price, then buy AI inference tokens. Sellers list capacity.
- The brand should feel **trusted to secure your future**. Key words: expertise, excellence, confidence. Precise, technical, assured, transparent.
- Who reads it, in priority order:
  1. Managers, CTOs and enterprise buyers.
  2. Experimental developers and AI-native startup engineers.
  3. AI agents buying on someone's behalf.
- **The story:** before the first request, a buyer can answer five questions:
  - what model
  - who operates it and where
  - what happens to the data
  - which security and delivery commitments apply
  - what exactly will be charged
- **The distinctions to show:** eligible vs. ineligible, declared vs. verified, estimated vs. billed.
- **Taglines:** "Inference. For those who know." · "Know the model. Know where it runs. Know what you pay."

## Hard constraints (MUST / NEVER)

### Truth
1. MUST take every figure, feature and claim from nytokenexchange.com or a cited source. Live figures, as of Nov 2026:
   - 675 models, 100 providers, 5.5% fee.
   - Token Index $380 per 1B tokens as of Oct 26, 2026.
   - Grades: AAA $1,794 · A $985 · B $219 · C $27.
2. MUST put anything unverified in brackets: `[N]`, `[date]`, `[Company]`, `[Name, role]`. Label sample data "Sample values".
3. NEVER invent customers, partners, certifications, quotes, metrics, trades or market activity.
4. MUST caption real people only with names, roles and quotes they have approved.
5. MUST escalate to a person rather than decide:
   - security or privacy claims
   - any use of the Verified Secure badge
   - pricing
   - customer or partner names
   - photos of real people
   - anything for press or regulators

### Voice
6. MUST write short, declarative headlines, then the concrete mechanism. Example: "Set the rules once. The exchange picks the route."
7. MUST name the control instead of making an assurance: "zero data retention", "fails closed", "attested build". NEVER write "fully secure", "enterprise-grade", "military-grade" or "bank-level".
8. MUST make limits easy to find: "Location: not proven by attestation." "Provisional. Not for trading."
9. MUST use the terms exactly as defined:
   - **declared:** the provider says so.
   - **verified:** NYTE checked the evidence.
   - **eligible / ineligible:** the route meets or fails the buyer's rules.
   - **estimated / billed**
   - **fails closed:** no qualifying route, so the request stops.
10. NEVER use crypto or hype words:
    - Crypto: coin, token sale, mint, wallet, moon, Web3, DeFi.
    - Hype: revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, unleash, supercharge, seamless, next-gen, best-in-class.
11. MUST name the company "NY Token Exchange" on first mention and "NYTE" after. NEVER write "NY Token Exchange (NYTE)". Provider and model names go in type, never as logos.

### Color
12. MUST use only the palette tokens:
    - Hero tier (≈80%): ink #151311, cream #FAF8F4, gray #F1EEE7, stone #DEDAD0, ink-2 #57514A.
    - Color tier (≈15%): purple #9B93F5, yellow #FFC800, lime #C9E265, sky #BFE3EC.
    - Functional tier (≈5%): orange #FF6B35, deep-purple #4B3FA8, green #2E3313.
    - Tints, for highlights only: lilac, yellow-tint, lime-tint, sky-tint.
13. MUST give every page or piece one **lead color** by family:
    - Market = purple: Home, Models & Providers, Exchange.
    - Price = yellow: Token Index, Reserved tokens, Buy.
    - Trust = lime: Trust, Security, Privacy, Status.
    - Knowledge = sky: Docs, Suppliers, Providers, stories.
14. MUST use at least two full-strength color sections besides the hero, using the other bright colors as accents: color bars, color fields, colored type on Ink. NEVER let cream or pastels dominate. NEVER reduce the palette to black, cream and purple.
15. MUST keep text on any color field in ink. Colored type is allowed only:
    - on Ink, in lime, sky, yellow or purple
    - in deep-purple on Cream, for one emphasized phrase
16. NEVER set purple, lime, yellow or orange as text on Cream.
17. MUST use orange only as the fill of the single primary button in a view.
18. NEVER use gradients, glows, glass, drop shadows, or tints as section grounds.
19. MUST keep every text pair at WCAG AA: 4.5:1, or 3:1 at 24 px and above.

### Type
20. MUST use ABC Pelikan only, in three voices:
    - **Sans** for prose: Light 300 for displays and figures, Regular 400 for body, Medium 500 for headings.
    - **Condensed Medium caps** with +10% tracking for labels, eyebrows and table heads.
    - **Mono** for prices, ids, hostnames and timestamps, with tabular figures.
21. SHOULD use Bold only with intention: one word, figure or line that must stand out, never as the default weight. NEVER use italics for emphasis, a second typeface family, or more than three sizes on one slide or ad.
22. Web scale:
    - display 104 / 72 / 48
    - heading 32 / 24 / 19
    - lead 22, body 17, small 14
    - label 13, data 14
    - Deck displays are 120 / 96 / 64.
    - Headlines are sentence case with tight tracking.

### Shape and graphics
23. MUST use square corners everywhere. The only round things are the globe, the live dot and illustration circles.
24. MUST build with lines:
    - rules of 1.5 px for structure, 2.5 px for table heads and chart lines, 4 px for eyebrow bars, 10 px for color bars
    - the **Band**: six stripe groups of 2 · 5 · 8 · 12 · 17 · 22 px on a 24 px pitch, thin to thick in the direction of travel, with **groups touching and no gap between groups**, once per page; NEVER squeezed or stretched, and left out of narrow formats such as skyscraper ads
    - the **Ladder**: equal stripes counting a real quantity
25. MUST place the logo, the Verified Secure badge, icons, Band patterns and illustrations from the asset files. NEVER redraw, retype, recolor beyond the approved versions, stretch, rotate or animate the logo.
26. Logo:
    - -ink on light grounds and color fields, -paper on Ink.
    - Clear space: half the mark's height on every side.
    - The NYTE wordmark is the default logo.
    - Minimum sizes: wordmark 56 px / 15 mm wide; lockup 96 px / 25 mm wide; mark 20 px / 6 mm.
27. Verified Secure badge:
    - Use that wording only.
    - Black on light grounds, white on Ink.
    - At least 64 px tall.
    - NEVER on a color field or inside a box.
    - Only where the claim is verifiable.
28. Icons:
    - 24 px grid, 1.5 px stroke, flat ends, sharp corners, in ink or cream.
    - Always paired with a word.
    - New icons follow the cut-line grammar: rails on a 4-unit pitch, slanted parallel strokes for motion, lines stopping short instead of meeting at a T, contours opened where a line passes through.
29. Illustration:
    - A circle is a place, a line is a flow (parallel lines slanted like the mark), and NYTE is the square at the center.
    - Flat, one ground plus ink plus at most two brand colors, one idea per picture. NEVER use the Band or stepped stripes in an illustration.
30. Photography:
    - The people behind the models, in color, natural light, at work.
    - NEVER stock, AI-generated people, black and white, screens as the subject, data centers, landscapes, handshakes or skylines.
    - Framed in a 1.5 px ink rule with a cream caption tab.

### Data and motion
31. MUST show source and as-of date under every chart and table. Line charts are 2.5 px and labeled at the line ends, with no legend when labels fit.
32. NEVER draw candlesticks, invented activity, pie or donut charts, 3D, or gradient fills. State must read without color: words, check and cross.
33. Motion:
    - Only data moves: ticker, chart draw, count-up, globe turn.
    - 160 / 320 / 800 ms with one easing curve, cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). Nothing bounces, floats or parallaxes.
    - MUST honor prefers-reduced-motion.

### Layout
34. Grids:
    - Web: 12 columns, 24 px gutter, 64 px margins, 1440 max, 128 px section padding.
    - Mobile: 4 columns, 16 px gutter, 20 px margins.
    - Deck: 1920 × 1080, 12 columns, 32 px gutter, 96 px margins.
35. Decks:
    - One idea per slide.
    - At most three sizes and two weights per slide, styled the same on every slide.
    - As few words as the idea needs; point to a source, site page or appendix for more.
    - Adjacent slides on different grounds.
    - Footer wordmark bottom-left, page number bottom-right in Mono.
36. NEVER produce crammed layouts, filler copy, generic card grids, everything-the-same-weight pages, or AI and tech tropes. That rules out pixel art, network meshes, brains, robots, space, glowing orbs, padlocks, coins and NY skylines.

## Output checklist (run before returning work)

- [ ] Every figure traced to the live site or bracketed; samples labeled.
- [ ] One lead color plus at least two full-strength color sections; text on color fields is ink.
- [ ] At most one orange button per view; no gradients or shadows; square corners.
- [ ] Only Pelikan in three voices; at most three sizes on any slide or ad.
- [ ] Band groups touch; logo, badge, icons and illustrations come from the files.
- [ ] All text pairs meet AA; focus visible; reduced motion respected.
- [ ] Anything on the escalation list (constraint 5) is flagged for a person.

When something is not covered, follow the intent of the closest rule and state what you assumed.
