# NYTE · Deck templates T01–T14

1920 × 1080 · 12 columns · 32 px gutter · 96 px margin. One idea per slide; adjacent slides on different grounds; ≤ 3 type sizes; footer: lockup left, deck label centre, page number right (Mono). Examples: Brand Guidelines V1, section 13, and `11_Reference_Work/NYTE_Investor_Briefing_Example.pdf`.

Sequence for a 14-slide briefing: lead field · Cream · Yellow · Ink · Cream · Sky · Ink · Cream · Ink · Lime · Cream · Cream · Purple · Photo.

## T01 · Cover

- **Use for:** Opens every deck. The tagline or the one-line promise.
- **Ground:** Lead colour field + Ink Band
- **Slots:** Lockup · eyebrow (deck · date · “not crypto”) · display-1 · lead
- **Avoid:** A second headline, a photo, or the badge.

## T02 · Question list

- **Use for:** The problem as the buyer’s questions.
- **Ground:** Cream
- **Slots:** display-2 with one deep-purple phrase · up to 5 numbered rows on colour blocks
- **Avoid:** More than five rows or full sentences in the rows.

## T03 · Big figures

- **Use for:** Market facts. Every figure carries its source.
- **Ground:** Bright field (yellow)
- **Slots:** display-2 · lead · up to 3 figures · source line
- **Avoid:** A figure without its source and date.

## T04 · Statement on Ink

- **Use for:** The one-sentence definition of NYTE.
- **Ground:** Ink
- **Slots:** display-1 with one coloured phrase · 3 columns with colour bars
- **Avoid:** Body copy longer than two lines per column.

## T05 · Mechanism

- **Use for:** How something works. One flow per slide.
- **Ground:** Cream
- **Slots:** display-2 left · diagram or illustration right · lead
- **Avoid:** Networks, arrows everywhere, or decoration.

## T06 · Spec cards

- **Use for:** The same fields side by side.
- **Ground:** Bright field (sky)
- **Slots:** display-2 · lead · 5 field cards with state chips · source
- **Avoid:** Hiding a declared field among verified ones.

## T07 · Evidence table

- **Use for:** Controls and evidence. Limits shown, not hidden.
- **Ground:** Ink
- **Slots:** display-2 · lead · table: layer, meaning, state · white badge if verifiable
- **Avoid:** The badge next to a claim NYTE has not verified.

## T08 · Five-up

- **Use for:** Product surfaces or options, never more than five.
- **Ground:** Cream
- **Slots:** display-2 · lead · up to 5 columns, each topped with a colour block
- **Avoid:** Cards with icons and bullets.

## T09 · Stats board

- **Use for:** Traction. Live figures or [brackets] only.
- **Ground:** Ink
- **Slots:** display-2 · figures in coloured type · bracketed placeholders
- **Avoid:** Unbracketed estimates or projected figures.

## T10 · Terms list

- **Use for:** Business model, pricing, terms.
- **Ground:** Bright field (lime)
- **Slots:** display-2 · lead · rows: figure, label, description
- **Avoid:** Fine print that changes the headline.

## T11 · Positioning

- **Use for:** Competition. Others named in type, never logos.
- **Ground:** Cream
- **Slots:** display-2 with deep-purple phrase · 2 × 2 with NYTE in the lead colour
- **Avoid:** Competitor logos or unsupported superlatives.

## T12 · Team

- **Use for:** Team and advisors. Names with consent only.
- **Ground:** Cream
- **Slots:** display-2 · lead · up to 4 portrait frames on colour blocks · names
- **Avoid:** Stock portraits or invented bios.

## T13 · The ask

- **Use for:** The raise or the request. One number.
- **Ground:** Lead colour field
- **Slots:** One figure-size amount · use-of-funds ladder rows
- **Avoid:** More than one number at hero size.

## T14 · Close

- **Use for:** Ends every deck. Contact and the not-crypto line.
- **Ground:** Photo + Ink panel + Band
- **Slots:** Paper lockup · display-2 with lime phrase · contact in Mono
- **Avoid:** A new message. Repeat the promise and stop.
