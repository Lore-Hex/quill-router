# NYTE Brand Book · V1

NY Token Exchange (NYTE) is the marketplace for buyers and sellers of inference. This system is the single source for every NYTE surface: website, product, decks, documents, ads, signage and swag. People and AI tools both build from it.

**Version 1 (Nov 2026).** Companion to *NYTE Brand Guidelines V1* (PDF). Where an older file disagrees, this one wins.

How to use this file: read **Foundation** and **Voice** before writing anything; read the section for each element before placing it. Rules carry IDs (`COL-03`) so a reviewer can cite them. MUST and NEVER are hard rules. SHOULD is the default; break it only with a stated reason.

---

## 1. Foundation

- **What NYTE is:** the marketplace for buyers and sellers of inference.
- **How it should feel:** *Trusted to secure your future.*
- **Taglines:** "Inference. For those who know." · "Know the model. Know where it runs. Know what you pay."
- **Key words:** Expertise · Excellence · Confidence. Attributes: precise, technical, assured, transparent, durable, market-leading.
- **Audience, in priority order:** (1) managers, CTOs and enterprise decision-makers; (2) experimental developers and AI-native startup engineers; (3) AI agents buying on someone's behalf, who need direct language, standard navigation and consistent structure.
- **The central story:** before the first request, a buyer can answer five questions — what model, who operates it and where, what happens to the data, which security and delivery commitments apply, what exactly will be charged. Show real distinctions: **eligible vs. ineligible, declared vs. verified, estimated vs. billed.**
- **What NYTE is not:** not a securities or cryptocurrency exchange; not a guarantee of capacity; not a regulator or certifier. "New York" is the market's name, not where requests run.

## 2. Voice

NYTE writes like a trading desk's best analyst: short, specific, unhurried.

- **VOICE-01 MUST** use short, declarative headlines, then follow the benefit with the concrete mechanism. "Set the rules once. The exchange picks the route."
- **VOICE-02 MUST** prefer named controls over broad assurances: "zero data retention", "fails closed", "attested build", never "fully secure" or "enterprise-grade".
- **VOICE-03 MUST** make limits easy to find: "Location: not proven by attestation." "Provisional. Not for trading."
- **VOICE-04 MUST** be checkable. Every figure is from the live site or a cited source; every unverified figure is a bracketed placeholder: `[N]`, `[date]`, `[Company]`.
- **VOICE-05 NEVER** use crypto or hype vocabulary: coin, mint, wallet, moon, Web3, DeFi; revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, unleash, supercharge, seamless.
- **VOICE-06 SHOULD** use exchange language where true: route, list, quote, spot price, supply, demand, settle, reserve.
- **Terms:** *declared* (the provider says so) · *verified* (NYTE checked the evidence) · *eligible / ineligible* (a route meets / fails the buyer's rules) · *estimated / billed* · *confidential compute (CC)* · *zero data retention (ZDR)* · *end-to-end encryption (E2EE)* · *fails closed* (no qualifying route, the request stops).
- **Names:** first mention **NY Token Exchange (NYTE)**, then **NYTE**. "Powered by TrustedRouter" where the endorsement is needed. Provider and model names in type, never as logos.

## 4. Colour

One palette, every colour with a job. Colour is not a garnish: every page carries several full-strength colour sections.

| Tier | Tokens | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | `ink`, `cream`, `gray`, `stone`, `ink-2` (`ink-2-dark`, `dark-rule` on Ink) | ≈ 80% (Cream is one ground among several, not the default) |
| Colour | `purple`, `yellow`, `lime`, `sky` — full strength | ≈ 15%, lead colour plus accents |
| Functional | `orange` (action), `deep-purple` (links, emphasis), `green` (verified ink) | ≈ 5% |
| Tints | `lilac`, `yellow-tint`, `lime-tint`, `sky-tint` | highlights only |

**Page theming (COL-01).** Every page belongs to a family and takes its **lead colour**: Market `purple` (Home, Models & Providers, Exchange) · Price `yellow` (Token Index, Reserved tokens, Buy) · Trust `lime` (Trust, Security, Privacy, Status) · Knowledge `sky` (Docs, Suppliers, Providers, launch stories). The lead colour takes the hero (as a full Field, or as the Band under a Cream hero), the eyebrow bars, selected tabs and one hero-scale element. See the PageThemes card.

**Accent hits (COL-02).** The other three bright colours appear as hits: colour bars over stats and guides, colour caption blocks under photos, full-strength section grounds (CTA, live globe, receipt, supplier block), and coloured type on Ink. A page SHOULD carry at least two full-strength colour sections besides its hero. Decks: give adjacent slides different grounds.

- **COL-03 MUST** keep text on any colour field in `ink`. Coloured type is allowed only on Ink (`lime`, `sky`, `yellow`, `purple`, all ≥ 7:1) and in `deep-purple` on Cream for one emphasised phrase.
- **COL-04 NEVER** set `purple` (2.5:1), `lime` (1.4:1), `orange` (2.7:1) or `yellow` as text on Cream.
- **COL-05 MUST** use `orange` only as the primary button fill, one per view.
- **COL-06 NEVER** use tints as section grounds, gradients, glows, glass, or drop shadows.
- **COL-07 MUST** keep every text pair at WCAG AA (4.5:1, 3:1 at 24 px+). Approved pairs: Ink on Cream 17.5 · Gray 16.0 · Stone 13.3 · Purple 7.0 · Lilac 13.5 · Yellow 11.9 · Lime 12.8 · Sky 13.6 · Orange 6.5. Ink 2 on Cream 7.4 · Stone 5.6. Deep purple on Cream 7.7 · Lilac 5.9. Green on Cream 12.4 · Lime 9.1. Cream on Ink 17.5; Ink 2 dark on Ink 8.8; Yellow 11.9 · Lime 12.8 · Sky 13.6 · Purple 7.0 on Ink.

## 5. Typography

One family, ABC Pelikan (the logo face, Dinamo), in three voices: **Sans is prose. Condensed is wayfinding. Mono is facts.** Licensed cuts: Pelikan Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500; Pelikan Condensed Medium; Pelikan Mono Regular and Medium. Bold is reserved for the wordmark.

- **TYPE-01 MUST** set display headlines in `display-1/2/3` (Light, tight tracking, sentence case). One `display-1` per page.
- **TYPE-02 MUST** set every eyebrow, table head and nav family title in `label` (Condensed caps, +10%).
- **TYPE-03 MUST** set prices, ids, hashes, hostnames and timestamps in `data` or `ticker` (Mono, tabular). Headline figures use `figure` (Sans Light, tabular).
- **TYPE-04 SHOULD** keep body to 45–75 characters, lead to 640 px.
- **Scale (web / deck / mobile):** display-1 104 / 120 / 48 · display-2 72 / 96 / 40 · display-3 48 / 64 / 32 · heading-1 32 / 40 · heading-2 24 · heading-3 19 · lead 22 / 30 · body 17 / 24 · small 14 · label 13 / 16 · data 14 · ticker 13. Displays scale with `clamp()` between 390 and 1440 px. Print: body 11 pt, captions 8.5 pt, labels 7.5 pt.
- **TYPE-05 NEVER** use italics for emphasis, more than three sizes on one slide or ad, or a second typeface family.

## 6. Graphic elements

**Everything is a line.** The mark is lines of different length; the Band is lines of different weight; the ticker, the chart and the rule are lines too. A new element joins the system only if it can be explained this way.

- **Band** (card: Band). Six stripe groups, 2 · 5 · 8 · 12 · 17 · 22 px on a 24 px pitch, thin to thick in the direction of travel. **GFX-01 MUST** keep the groups touching: no gap between groups; the spacing inside a group never changes. Once per page, full-bleed. Assets: `Patterns/band-*.svg`.
- **Ladder** (card: Ladder). Equal stripes counting a real quantity, always next to its number. Index grades: C `yellow`, B `lime`, A `sky`, AAA `purple`.
- **Rule.** 1.5 px structure; 2.5 px table heads and chart lines; 4 px eyebrow bar; 10 px colour bar. Rules replace boxes and shadows. Square corners everywhere (`radius-none`).
- **Ticker** (card: Ticker). The only element that moves on its own.
- **Live globe** (card: LiveGlobe). The rotating dot globe on Sky.
- **Verified Secure badge** (card: Badge). Black on light, white on Ink, never on a colour field or in a box, ≥ 64 px tall, only where the claim is verifiable.

## 7. Iconography

Assets: `Icons/` (43). Card-free: icons support labels, they never stand alone.

- **Construction (from the mark):** 24 px grid, live area 2–22, stroke 1.5 px, **flat ends and sharp corners**. Horizontals sit on the mark's rails — a 4-unit pitch (y 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20) — so a 16-unit circle spans rail to rail, exactly as the globe sits between the mark's top and bottom lines. No fills except the NYTE square (a solid square used as a node or marker) and the live dot.
- **R1 Rails.** Lists, stacks and data marks are parallel rails on the 4-unit pitch.
- **R2 Slant.** Where an icon means movement, flow or speed (agent, live, fail-closed, menu, billed, exchange, hyperscaler; globe and latency carry the original speed lines), its parallel strokes keep equal length and shift sideways rail by rail — the mark's italic motion. Never staggered lengths.
- **R3 Cut.** Lines never meet at a T: one stroke stops 1.25 short of the other (developer, enterprise, sovereign, download). Contours open where a line passes through them (search, region, ineligible, info), and boxes open at corners or edges (attested, gpu, receipt).
- **R4 Pass.** Lines may run past the shape they belong to, as the mark's rails run past the globe (region, receipt, ineligible).
- **Fixed drawings:** `globe`, `model`, `application`, `latency` and `route` keep their original drawing, round ends included. Treat them as fixed; do not redraw them in the cut-line grammar.
- **ICN-01 MUST** draw icons in `ink`, or `cream` on Ink (inline the SVG to recolour). One highlighted icon may take `lime` on Ink.
- **ICN-02 MUST** pair every icon with a word. Sizes: 16 (inline), 24 (default), 32 and 48 for diagrams, with the stroke scaling proportionally.
- **ICN-03 NEVER** use emoji, filled icon sets, third-party logos, padlocks, robots or brains. State icons (`verified`, `declared`, `ineligible`, `estimate`, `billed`) mean exactly their term.
- **ICN-04 MUST** apply at least one of R2–R4 to every new icon (the five fixed drawings above are the only exceptions); a closed generic pictogram is off-brand. Draw new icons with the generator rules above, not from a stock set.

## 8. Illustration

Assets: `Illustrations/` (10). Flat, bold and simple. One idea per picture.

- **Grammar:** a **circle** is a place (buyer, provider, region, model); a **line** is a flow (request, route, payment), drawn as parallel lines slanted like the mark's speed lines; **NYTE is always the square, at the centre.** Quantities are ladders of equal bars.
- **Construction:** 480 × 336 artboard on a 24 px grid; shapes snap to 12 px; lines 6 px, round caps.
- **Colour:** one ground (a bright colour, Cream or Ink), `ink`, plus at most two other brand colours. Ineligible things are `stone` with a cross; verified things are `lime` with a check; declared things are dashed.
- **ILL-01 MUST** say one idea per picture and carry its own `aria-label`. Labels, when needed, are set beside it in `label` or `data`, not drawn in.
- **ILL-02 NEVER** use the Band or stepped stripes, gradients, 3D, isometrics, glows, dotted networks, robots, brains, padlocks, coins or skylines in an illustration.
- Set: 01 exchange · 02 routing · 03 region · 04 declared → verified · 05 estimated → billed · 06 grades · 07 fails closed · 08 receipt · 09 agent within budget · 10 supply meets demand. New ones follow the same grid and colour rules.

## 9. Photography

The people behind the models: founders, engineers, operators and customers doing the work. Colour only. A few strong moments per surface (a full-bleed event photo, a founder story, launch cards), never a gallery.

**Art direction for commissioned shoots**
- Light: daylight or practical light in the subject's own space; soft, directional, warm-neutral. No studio seamless, no flash glare, no coloured gels.
- Colour: natural skin, true whites, saturated but not pushed. Keep the setting's own colours; avoid blue-heavy grades.
- Framing: eye level or slightly below; people mid-conversation, at a whiteboard, around a table, on a stage — looking at each other or the work, not the lens (portraits excepted). Leave clean space for the caption tab bottom-left.
- Wardrobe: what they wear to work. Solid colours over logos; no third-party branding front and centre.
- Setting: offices, labs, facility floors, events. **No screens as the subject, no server rooms or data-centre aisles, no moody landscapes, no handshakes or staged boardrooms.**
- Posing: direct groups loosely; shoot through a moment rather than lining people up, except one honest team photo per story.

**Selection and treatment for partner and third-party photos**
- Accept: colour, sharp, at least 2000 px on the long edge, real people at work or in their team, consent on file, publication rights cleared. Reject: stock, AI-generated people, black-and-white, heavy filters, screens or server rooms as the subject, visible third-party marks you cannot clear.
- Crop: square-cornered to the layout's ratio (16:9 full-bleed, 3:2 cards, 4:5 portraits); keep eyes in the top third; never crop through heads at the forehead.
- Treat: neutralise white balance, lift shadows gently, match contrast across a set; no colour casts, vignettes or grain added. Never recolour into a brand tint.
- Frame: inside a 1.5 px `ink` rule, with a Cream caption tab ("[Company] · [City]" or name and role with consent), or above a colour caption block (LaunchCard).
- **PHO-01 NEVER** attach invented names, quotes or claims to real people in a photo.

## 10. Data visualisation

- **DATA-01 MUST** show real data only, with source and as-of date under every chart and table. Interpolated shapes are labelled as such; sample values are labelled "Sample values".
- **DATA-02 NEVER** draw decorative candlesticks, invented market activity, pie or donut charts, 3D or gradient fills.
- Lines: 2.5 px; series 1 `ink` (or the lead colour on Ink), series 2 the lead colour, series 3 `text-2`. Label lines at their ends; no legend when labels fit. One baseline rule; 1 px `rule` gridlines.
- Bars and grades: the Ladder. Tables: the DataTable. Figures: the Stat.
- State always reads without colour: chip form (filled / outline / stone), check and cross, words.

## 11. Motion

- Only data moves: the ticker scrolls, a chart draws, a figure counts, the globe turns, a trade pings. Decoration never animates; a Band may reveal once.
- One direction: left to right, or rising. Nothing bounces, floats or parallaxes.
- Timing: `duration-ui` 160 ms · `duration-reveal` 320 ms · `duration-data` 800 ms · ticker 60 px/s after a 2 s pause · one curve, `ease-standard`.
- **MOT-01 MUST** honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: static ticker, complete charts, final figures, a still globe, colour-only hovers.
- Focus: 2 px solid `ink` (`cream` on Ink) outline, 2 px offset, never removed.

## 14. Rules for AI tools

- **AI-01** Read this file first, then the card for every component you place.
- **AI-02** Place logos, the badge, icons, patterns and illustrations from the asset files. Never draw, retype or approximate them.
- **AI-03** Use tokens by name. Never introduce a colour, font, radius, shadow or gradient that is not here.
- **AI-04** Give each page one lead colour by its family and at least two full-strength colour sections; keep text on colour fields in `ink`.
- **AI-05** Keep the Band's groups touching; never add a gap between groups.
- **AI-06** Use figures from the live site only; bracket anything unverified. Label sample data.
- **AI-07** Escalate to a person, do not decide: security or privacy claims, any use of the badge, pricing, customer or partner names, photography of real people, anything for press or regulators.
- **AI-08** Say "not a securities or cryptocurrency exchange" wherever NYTE is introduced to a new audience.
- **AI-09** When something is not covered, follow the closest rule's intent and state what you assumed.
