# Icons

43 line icons on a 24 px grid (live area 2–22), 1.5 px stroke, flat ends, sharp corners, drawn in Ink `#151311`. Inline the SVG and set `stroke` (and the fill of the NYTE square or live dot) to `cream` on Ink grounds; `<img>` cannot recolour.

The set is cut from the mark: horizontals sit on its rails (4-unit pitch), movement icons use its slant (parallel strokes of equal length shifted rail by rail), lines stop short instead of meeting at a T, contours open where a line passes through, and lines may run past their shape as the rails run past the globe. Five icons keep their original drawing, round ends included: `globe`, `model`, `application`, `latency` and `route`.

State icons (verified, declared, ineligible, estimate, billed) mean exactly their term. Always pair an icon with a word. Sizes 16 / 24 / 32 / 48, stroke scaling with the size.
